How the copy book is scored

Performers assume you bought and sold the same share count on the insider’s trade dates, at the first close on or after each trade date from the same prices/ file used to mark leftovers. A reported Form 4/SEDI fill is only a check that the print belongs on that tape: if it is more than 50% from that close, the lot is skipped (dual-listed or ADR mismatch). Same-day fills on one name that disagree by more than 50% keep only the cluster within 20% of the close. Sales close the oldest lots first. Open leftovers are marked to the last close on file. Rank is total dollar P&L. Percent is P&L ÷ cash spent on buys. Filers with under $1,000 of priced buys are skipped.

Active traders are ranked by print count. Active names are ranked by how many officer prints hit that ticker. Not a recommendation. Not the officer’s actual P&L — they may have other lots, options, and taxes.
